# WorkPlanX Website — Project Rules

- This is a single-page landing website; keep the structure focused and responsive.
- The only primary CTA is **Contact us**. Do not add pricing or demo booking.
- Approved brand guidelines in `docs/brand/` take precedence over generated styles or suggestions.
- Xero is the only integration that may be named; do not invent capabilities, claims, customers, or proof.
- Keep all website copy in `src/content/site.ts`.
- Review `docs/01_website_layout_and_structure.md` and `docs/02_business_context.md` before changing structure or business messaging.
- Keep components typed, reusable, accessible, and maintainable. Separate business logic from presentation.
- Run relevant checks after implementation and review existing file contents before changing them.

# WorkPlanX Landing Page: Layout and Structure

## 1. Goal and principles

**Goal:** Show WorkPlanX in action through real product work, and get the visitor to **Contact us**.

| Principle | What it means |
|---|---|
| One page, short | Single scrolling landing page. 7 sections. No sub-pages. |
| Show, don't list | The capability is vast. Use real product screenshots, not feature walls. |
| One CTA | **Contact us** is the only primary action everywhere. |
| Clean | Lots of whitespace, large screenshots, short copy (max 2 lines per card). |
| Responsive | Desktop, tablet, mobile. Sections stack cleanly. |

**Out of scope (not available):** demo booking, pricing, blog, multi-page navigation.

**Design reference:** odoo.com. Take the clean hero, large product visuals, tabbed feature showcase and plenty of whitespace. Do not copy its size or depth. Follow approved WorkPlanX brand guidelines (logo, colours, typography).

---

## 2. Page map

```
[ Sticky Header ]
1. Hero
2. Problem strip
3. How it works (5 steps)

6. Proof (testimonials / logos)
7. Contact us (final CTA + form)
[ Footer ]
```

## 3. Section details

### Sticky header
- Left: WorkPlanX logo
- Centre (anchor links): How it works · Our work · Portal & Mobile
- Right: **Admin Portal login** and **Customer Portal login** (text links, secondary) + **Contact us** (primary button)
- Mobile: logo + Contact us button + hamburger

### 1. Hero
- **Headline (draft):** One platform for your entire project lifecycle.
- **Subheadline (draft):** From workforce and daily operations to reporting, waste management, payroll and invoicing, all in one place.
- **CTAs:** **Contact us** (primary) · See how it works (text link, scrolls to section 3)
- **Visual:** Large real screenshot (Roster or Dashboard) in a browser frame, with a small mobile app screenshot overlapping.
- **Trust line under hero:** "Built for labour-based, site-delivered services, with a specialist waste and resource-recovery core."

### 2. Problem strip
Four compact items in a row (icon + one line each). Message: better coordination, visibility, streamlined workflows.
- "We don't know if we're making money on this job."
- "Payroll takes days and still goes wrong."
- "We invoice late and miss variations."
- "Our clients keep calling for updates."

Closing line: **WorkPlanX puts it all in one place.**

### 3. How it works (5 steps)
Horizontal flow on desktop, vertical on mobile. Icon, title, one line each.

| Step | Title | One-liner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan | Register projects, win work, set up people and clients. |
| 2 | Roster | Assign crews and vehicles to sites each day. |
| 3 | Capture | Field teams record hours, reports, dockets and weigh tickets on mobile. |
| 4 | Pay and bill | Hours flow to timesheets and payroll. Work flows to invoices and Xero. |
| 5 | Share | Clients see projects, dockets, waste performance and invoices in their portal. |

### 4. Our work: product showcase (main section)
This is the core of the page. One large tabbed viewer with real screenshots.

**Layout:** Tab list on the left (desktop) or a horizontal scroll of tabs (mobile). Large screenshot on the right. Each tab has a title, one sentence and 2 to 3 short bullets.

| Tab | Screenshot | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Roster and tasks | Roster | Plan the day: people, vehicles, sites. |
| Daily reports | Project Daily Report | Hours and tonnes recorded once, used everywhere. |
| Waste and resource recovery | Weigh Scale and Resource Recovery Report | Recycling and recovery reporting by project, customer and workspace. |
| Payroll | Payroll | Award-based loadings and penalty rates applied automatically. |
| Invoicing and Xero | Contract Invoice | Bill from claims or dockets. Sync with Xero both ways. |

Keep to 5 tabs. Do not add more.

### 5. One platform, three access points
Three equal cards, each with a screenshot.

| Card | For | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Web (Admin Portal) | Office teams and managers | Projects, rostering, reports, payroll, invoicing. |
| Mobile App | Field workers and drivers | Shifts, hours, daily reports, weigh tickets, photos. |
| Customer Portal | Clients | Projects, docket sign-off, waste performance, invoices. |

Small integration line below: **Integrates with Xero.** Add other integrations only once confirmed.

## 4. CTA rules

- Primary button text everywhere: **Contact us**
- Appears in: header, hero, after the showcase, final section
- All Contact us buttons scroll to the form in section 7, or open it in a modal
- No pricing, demo booking or "Get Started" buttons
- Login links are plain text links, never styled as primary

---

## 5. Responsive behaviour

| Element | Desktop | Tablet | Mobile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | Full nav | Full nav | Logo, Contact us, hamburger |
| Hero | Text left, visual right | Text above, visual below | Stacked |
| Problem strip | 4 across | 2 x 2 | Stacked |
| How it works | Horizontal | Horizontal | Vertical |
| Showcase | Tabs left, image right | Tabs above | Scrollable tabs above |
| Access points | 3 cards | 3 cards or 1 + 2 | Stacked |

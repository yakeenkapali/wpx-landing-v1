# WorkPlanX: Business Context

Source: Business Capability Overview (Dohoro Management Pvt Ltd, August 2026) and the website requirement questionnaire. Website: www.workplanx.com

---

## 1. What WorkPlanX is

An **end-to-end operations and billing platform for labour-based, site-delivered services**, with a **specialist waste and resource-recovery core**.

A business sets up its people, clients and projects. Every day it rosters crews and vehicles to sites, captures what happened through daily reports and dockets, weighs and classifies waste, turns hours into timesheets and payroll, turns work into claims and invoices, pushes invoices to Xero, and gives clients a self-service portal.

Everything is separated by **workspace**, so one company can run multiple branches, entities or regions with their own people, rates, clients, numbering and branding.

## 2. Core message

> One platform to manage the entire project lifecycle: workforce, daily operations, reporting, waste management, payroll and invoicing.

Emphasise: **better coordination, improved visibility, streamlined workflows, centralised project management.**

## 3. Audience

- **Primary:** Businesses that manage projects, workforce, field operations and related admin, especially construction and project-based businesses.
- **Buyers/evaluators:** owners, operations managers, finance leads.
- **Users:** admins, managers, supervisors, office/finance staff, estimators, field employees and drivers, subcontractors, and customers (via portal).

## 4. Business problems it solves

| Problem | How WorkPlanX solves it |
|---|---|
| "We don't know if we're making money on this job." | Budget vs actual vs variation hours live on every project. |
| "The client says we didn't do that work." | Dockets signed by the client, versioned, PDF'd, audit-logged. Disputes tracked. |
| "Payroll takes days and we still get it wrong." | Field hours captured once, flow to timesheet, approval and pay run with Award loadings applied. Exports to Xero. |
| "We invoice late and miss variations." | Approved claims flagged ready to invoice. Variations tracked separately. |
| "The client wants a recycling report every month." | Weigh tickets roll up into project, customer and workspace Resource Recovery reports. |
| "Our client keeps calling for updates." | Customer Portal gives self-service access to projects, dockets, waste data, invoices. |
| "We can't give everyone access to everything." | Dynamic per-user, per-module, per-action permissions. |
| "We run three branches and they all work differently." | Workspaces isolate people, clients, rates, numbering and settings. |
| "Finance and operations are separate worlds." | Xero holds the ledger. WorkPlanX holds operations. Two-way sync. |

## 5. The business flow (the spine of the product)

Know people and clients → register and win projects → plan and claim tasks → prove what happened (daily reports, dockets, weigh tickets) → evidence pays staff, bills the client and reports waste recovery → client sees it all in the portal.

Key principle: **"If it isn't in a report, it can't be paid and it can't be billed."**

## 6. Capabilities by area

### Foundation
- **Workspaces:** one per branch, entity or region.
- **Workforce:** Admins, Managers, Employees, Subcontractors. Onboarding by invite, device approval (anti-fraud), OH&S inductions, status history.
- **Clients:** customers, prospects, contacts, customer portal users, client-specific pricing.
- **Permissions:** role tiers plus dynamic per-module, per-action access. Full activity/audit log.

### Demand
- **Project Register:** sales pipeline (Ongoing, Won, Unsuccessful, Archived), estimates, recurring follow-ups, win/loss reasons.
- **Projects:** site geo-location and radius, team, budget vs actual hours, rate overrides, document drive, inductions.

### Delivery
- **Task and Claims:** claimable tasks, variations/change orders, approval workflow, claim to invoice.
- **Roster:** daily plan with crews, supervisors, drivers, fleet allocation, day sheet lifecycle.
- **Reports:** Project Daily Report (hours and waste), Daily Report, Supervisor Report, Docket Report, Tipping Reports, configurable report templates.

### Waste management (specialist edge)
- **Config:** load types, waste types with recovery pathways, vehicles, tipping yards.
- **Capture:** weigh scale (on site), weigh bridge (third-party facilities), waste requests from clients.
- **Reporting:** material composition, recovery pathway distribution, recycling percentage, volumes. By project, customer, workspace. Issued as formal Resource Recovery Reports.

### Pay and bill
- **Timesheets:** employee and subcontractor streams, approval workflow, remote approval links.
- **Payroll:** built to Australian payroll standards (Award/EA/NES). Casual loading, overtime, shift and night loadings, weekend and public holiday penalties, allowances, break rules, leave accruals. Weekly or fortnightly pay runs. Export to Xero and Excel.
- **Invoicing:** item and rate price book (default, customer, project levels), Contract Invoice (from claims), Docket Invoice (from signed dockets), Xero Invoice sync.

### Access points
- **Admin Portal (web):** office teams and managers. Also super-admin control of workspaces and maintenance windows.
- **Mobile App:** shifts, hours, daily report submission, weigh tickets, photos. Device approval and forced-update control.
- **Customer Portal:** projects, docket sign-off and disputes, waste summaries, waste requests, invoices (finance permission gated), finance dashboard, client user management, activity log.

### Supporting
Warehousing, tools and equipment, fleet register, OH&S inductions, notifications (in-app, email, SMS), calendar, messaging, support desk, audit log, cookie consent.

## 7. Integrations

- **Xero (confirmed):** contacts, GL codes, tax rates and tracking categories synced in. Invoices exported out and synced back. Timesheet and payroll data flows to Xero.
- No other integrations are confirmed in the source documents. Do not claim others on the website.

## 8. Differentiators

1. Waste and resource-recovery reporting built into the operations flow.
2. Hours captured once, used for payroll, billing and reporting. No re-keying.
3. Award-aware payroll calculation (Australian standards).
4. Client-facing portal with separate, granular permissions.
5. Multi-workspace structure for multi-branch businesses.
6. Full audit trail across internal and client actions.

## 9. Messaging guidelines

**Do**
- Lead with the whole lifecycle in one place.
- Use plain business language: roster, dockets, payroll, invoices, waste reports.
- Show real screens.
- Keep claims to what the capability overview confirms.

**Don't**
- Don't list every module on the landing page.
- Don't show pricing or promise demo booking (not available).
- Don't name integrations other than Xero.
- Don't over-use glossary terms (PDR, Weigh Bridge) without a plain explanation.

## 11. Glossary (short)

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Workspace | A business unit or branch. The container for all data. |
| Project Register | Sales pipeline before a job is won. |
| Project | A won job. All hours, waste, dockets, invoices attach to it. |
| Claim | Completed work claimed to the client for payment. |
| Variation | Extra scope tracked and billed separately. |
| Roster / Day Sheet | The daily plan of who and what goes to which site. |
| PDR | Project Daily Report: hours worked and waste removed. |
| Docket | Job record signed by the client, billed on what was done. |
| Weigh Scale / Weigh Bridge | Waste weighed on site / at a third-party facility. |
| Resource Recovery Report | Recycling and recovery performance report for a client. |
| Customer Portal | Client's own permissioned login. |
